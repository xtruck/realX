Microphone

Off unless you choose one. Sound is analysed in this browser and never sent anywhere. Turning it on or off reloads this page, because the browser only grants the microphone to a page that asks for it.

Quality

Low is the lightest, with rougher hands and fingers. Balanced is the best starting point. High gives sharper hands and fingers but is slower, so the arms update less often on a modest computer.

Outfit

Changing the camera, microphone, quality or outfit restarts the camera and reloads the tracker for a moment.

Camera view

Desk view follows your head, face, arms, hands and fingers and ignores the hips and legs, which the desk hides. If an arm or hand drops out of the picture (onto the keyboard) it stays where it was for a moment, then rests on the desk.
